# Product Requirements Document for AI News Podcast App

## Product Vision and Mission Statement
The AI News Podcast app aims to be a leading source for AI enthusiasts, providing timely and insightful news coverage in an engaging podcast format. Our mission is to seamlessly connect users with the latest developments in artificial intelligence, enriching their understanding and appreciation of the field.

## Feature List with Detailed Descriptions

1. **User Registration and Login**
   - **Description:** Users can create an account using their email or social media logins. 
   - **Functionality:** Confirmation email is sent upon successful registration.

2. **AI Research Integration**
   - **Description:** A multi-agent AI system aggregates and analyzes the latest AI news every hour.
   - **Functionality:** Updates the news database to ensure users receive current content.

3. **Podcast Creation Interface**
   - **Description:** An intuitive interface for editors to create, edit, and publish podcasts.
   - **Functionality:** Allows saving drafts and live publishing capabilities.

4. **User Feedback System**
   - **Description:** Users rate and provide feedback on podcast episodes.
   - **Functionality:** Feedback visibility for both users and the editing team.

5. **Search Functionality**
   - **Description:** Users can search for specific topics within the app.
   - **Functionality:** Returns results from both recent and archived episodes.

6. **Personalized Recommendations**
   - **Description:** Suggests podcasts based on user interests and listening history.
   - **Functionality:** Uses algorithms to tailor recommendations.

## User Stories with Acceptance Criteria

1. **User Registration**
   - As a user, I want to register with my email so that I can create an account.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** User receives a confirmation email after successful registration.

2. **Listening to Podcasts**
   - As a user, I want to listen to curated podcasts on AI news so that I am informed about the latest trends.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** Podcasts load within 3 seconds and are available for streaming.

3. **Providing Feedback**
   - As a user, I want to provide feedback on podcasts to express my opinion.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** Feedback appears in the system and is viewable to the editing team.

## Feature Prioritization (MoSCoW Method)

- **Must Have**
  - User Registration and Login
  - AI Research Integration
  - Podcast Creation Interface

- **Should Have**
  - User Feedback System
  - Search Functionality

- **Could Have**
  - Personalized Recommendations
  - Social Sharing Features

- **Won't Have**
  - Live Streaming of Content (at this stage)
